About Me

One dependable person for strategy, content and community.

I'm Kelvin, a social media manager who came into this work through the operations side, which is exactly why brands stay consistent once I take over the calendar. I don't just post; I plan pillars, brief the visuals, write the captions, schedule everything, and stay in the comments so the audience feels answered.

Right now I coordinate social media for small business clients, design in Canva to keep a recognisable look across platforms, and teach digital productivity and content workflows to students at Mediacrest Training College. How I Work

From brand brief to published feed

01

02

Plan & Brief

Pillars, formats and dates go into one calendar. Every post gets a brief before anything is designed.

03

Create & Publish

Graphics in Canva, captions written, approvals collected, posts scheduled ahead of the week, not during it.

04

Engage & Report

Daily comment and DM replies, then a monthly report with clear recommendations for the next cycle.
